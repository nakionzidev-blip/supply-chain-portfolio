# LogiStock | Supply Chain Data Analytics

**Python · NumPy · Logistics Analytics · Inventory Management**

## Overview

LogiStock is a supply chain analytics project focused on extracting operational insights from warehouse inventory, shipment activity, and delivery performance data.

The project explores how numerical data processing can support inventory monitoring, identify potential stock shortages, analyze delivery delays, and improve visibility into logistics operations.

The analysis is built around a warehouse environment comprising 12 product families, four storage zones, 24 months of shipment activity, and 200 delivery records.

## Business Objectives

- **Inventory monitoring:** identify stock shortages and products below predefined alert thresholds.
- **Shipment analysis:** examine monthly activity and compare performance across two years.
- **Delivery performance:** identify late deliveries and investigate relationships between delivery time, distance, weight, and cost.
- **Operational visibility:** consolidate key findings into a logistics warehouse overview.

## Technical Approach

The project uses NumPy to structure and process numerical data through:

- Multidimensional arrays and vectorized operations.
- Array indexing, slicing, and reshaping.
- Conditional searches with `np.where()`.
- Boolean filtering for operational segmentation.
- Numerical summaries and visual analysis with Matplotlib.

## Data Overview

| Dataset | Scope | Analytical purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Warehouse inventory | 12 products × 4 zones | Stock monitoring and shortage detection |
| Delivery records | 200 deliveries | Delay, distance, weight, and cost analysis |
| Monthly shipments | 24 months | Activity trends and year-over-year comparison |

The datasets are generated by the project notebook for analytical and demonstration purposes.
